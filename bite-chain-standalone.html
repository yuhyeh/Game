🐭 Mouse eats 🐍 Snake · 🐍 Snake eats 🐱 Cat · 🐱 Cat eats 🐭 Mouse

Setup

60-card deck: 20 Mouse, 20 Cat, 20 Snake. 4 players — you (🦝) and three bots (🦊 🦉 🐺). Each player is dealt 3 cards to start. You only see your own hand; the bots' hands stay face-down.

Turn sequence

  1. Draw — each player draws one card, clockwise starting with you.
  2. Play — tap a card in your hand to select it, then press Play. Bots pick automatically. All 4 cards reveal at once.
  3. Battle — the round's winner(s) are worked out and scored.

Repeat Draw → Play → Battle. Once the deck can't fill a full round, drawing stops automatically and you keep playing straight from your hands until every player's hand is empty.

Winning a round

  1. The animal played the fewest times that round wins.
  2. If two animals tie for fewest, the predator of the two wins (e.g. Snake beats Cat if both are tied).
  3. If all 4 players play the same animal, there's no winner — the result carries forward and is decided by whichever animal/predator wins the next round. That round's winner(s) collect points for both rounds.

End of game

The game ends when every player's hand is completely empty. Highest score wins. If you're tied for the top score you'll see "You are awesome!" — otherwise, "Better try next time." Restart anytime from the Game tab.
